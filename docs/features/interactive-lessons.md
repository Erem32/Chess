# Interactive lessons

Twenty-four lessons explain piece movement, special moves, king safety and basic tactics. Each includes a single-move exercise and feedback explaining the idea. The server distinguishes illegal moves from legal incorrect answers. Learners can retry or leave, and the marker setting applies. Lesson cards show topic labels and are accessible from the Play dashboard.

The check-blocking lesson shows a bishop protected by a knight, including why capturing the bishop loses the attacking rook. The underpromotion lesson contrasts stalemate after promoting to a queen with a forced checkmate after promoting to a rook.

Lesson content and its format are in [practice data](../../backend/app/data/README.md). See `test_learning_content.py` and the `test:e2e:learning` browser check.
