# Teaching examples

Twenty-five examples use prepared positions, move-by-move explanations and a read-only board to demonstrate piece movement, special rules, king safety, tactics, opening principles and endgames. They are grouped by topic, accessible from the Play dashboard, and do not change saved games.

Some examples intentionally demonstrate a mistake, such as allowing a quick checkmate or accidentally stalemating the opponent; their descriptions identify the consequence. The check-blocking and underpromotion examples show the continuation that makes the lesson meaningful.

See the [content format](../../backend/app/data/TEACHING.md), `test_teaching_api.py`, and the `test:e2e:teaching` browser check.
